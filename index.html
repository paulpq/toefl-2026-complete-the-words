About the 2026 format & official practice

ETS describes “Complete the Words” as a short academic passage of about 70–100 words, with an intact first sentence and 10 partially missing words. Following that sentence, the second half of every second word is generally removed.

These 10 passages are original practice written to follow that pattern. They are not official ETS test questions, and the practice score is not a TOEFL score prediction. The answer key explains the intended completion for each blank.
